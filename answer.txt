a. What are some Pros and Cons of Agile development (vs non-Agile)? List 2 of each.
Some pros of Agile are that it is an iterative approach with constant collaboration with the customers and that it provides consistent value adds to the customer.
Some cons of Agile are that there is additional overhead caused by structured meetings and that developers may lose the "flow" of getting work done because of intermitten project management and oversight as opposed to a waterfall approach.

b. Explain the role and responsibilities of the Scrum Master vs the Product Owner.
The Scrum Master facilitates all Scrum specific activities like JIRA and sprints.
The Product Owner sets direction for the team and manages the interactions with stakeholders.

c. What are the 3 types of meetings that happen during a sprint cycle? List at least 2 points on what needs to be discussed during each of these meetings.
There is the Sprint Planning Meeting in which it is determined which stories will be completed, how hard they are, and who will do them.
There are Daily Scrum meetings in which team members discuss work completed, their plan for the day, and any blockers they have encountered.
There is a Sprint Retrospective Meeting in which members discuss work completed and incomplete stories are moved back to the backlog.

d. Differentiate between Epic, Story, Bug, and Task issue types in JIRA
An Epic is the largest block within the hierarchy of JIRA organization. It is a large body of work to be broken down into smaller stories.
A Story is a specific software requirement expressed within a few sentences. Stories are usually focused around business value. They address a requirement of the project.
A Task is a more specific or smaller issue than a story. Tasks can be to-dos that are not necessarily user facing or complete business requirements.
A Bug is a problem with the code that needs to be fixed.
